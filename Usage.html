How to use STR-WISE

Search STR-WISE

First search for the genus of interest here. STR-WISE can be filtered according to your needs and wishes, e.g. maximum amplicon size, The full STR sheet can be downloaded here.

The genera analysed in STR-WISE can also be filtered for specific below.

Laboratory validation

Now its your turn, order your primers, test them in your lab, validate them according to forensic standards, start you database and solve wildlife forensic cases!

Can't find your genus in our database?

We also analyse genomes of non-CITES-listed genera upon request. Contact us to suggest a genus or genome for inclusion in our STR analysis pipeline!
