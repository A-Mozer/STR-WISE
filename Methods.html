Methods

Pipeline

1. Genomes
For each CITES-listed genus with at least one genome assembly available in NCBI, the best assembly is selected as the reference genome. Other usable assemblies from the same genus are used as comparison genomes. If the reference lacks a sex chromosome present in other assemblies (e.g. a Y chromosome in a female reference), the best available genome carrying that chromosome is used for marker discovery on that chromosome.

2. STR Detection
PERF is used to identify tetranucleotide STRs with 7–30 repeat units. Loci are excluded if another STR occurs within the same amplicon.

3. Primer Design
Primer3 designs primer pairs with an expected amplicon length of 100–500 bp.

4. Comparison Across Genomes
Amplicons are BLAST-searched against other genomes within the genus to identify additional alleles and assess STR variability.

5. Marker Selection Criteria
Candidate markers are evaluated against the selection criteria described below. Markers are retained in the database regardless of linkage or amplicon overlap. STR-WISE therefore provides a pool of candidate markers from which users can select suitable combinations for their own panels.

5. Multiplexing
Primer pairs are screened for potential off-target amplification products in the reference genome and the human genome. PrimerPooler is then used to assign markers to at least two multiplex pools while avoiding primer-dimer interactions.

6. STR-WISE Databaseg
Loci are retained in the STR-WISE database regardless of linkage or amplicon overlap. STR-WISE therefore provides a pool of candidate markers from which users can select suitable combinations for their own panels.
