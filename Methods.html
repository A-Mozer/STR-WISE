Methods

Pipeline

Genomes
For every CITES-listed genus with an NCBI genome assembly, the best assembly (RefSeq reference > assembly level > contig N50) is the reference; all other usable assemblies of the genus (contig N50 ≥ 10 kb, plausible genome size) serve as comparison genomes. If the reference lacks a sex chromosome present in other genomes (e.g. Y in a female reference), markers for that chromosome are taken from the best genome carrying it.

STR detection. PERF; tetranucleotide repeats only (mono-/dinucleotide motifs removed), 7–30 repeat units, longest perfect block ≥ 5 units, compound repeats with gaps ≤ 10 bp merged. Loci with another tetranucleotide STR within 100 bp or another SSR within 20 bp are removed; 300 bp flanks must be free of N..

Primer design. Primer3: amplicon 100–450 bp, primer 18–27 bp (opt. 21), Tm 57–63 °C (opt. 60, ΔTm ≤ 3), GC 30–65 %, no 3′ end in repeat-masked sequence or on another SSR. Three primer pairs per locus are evaluated.

Single copy. Amplicon windows mapping a second time to the reference (≥ 50 % length, ≥ 85 % identity) are removed. Primer pairs whose amplicon contains a second tetranucleotide STR (≥ 7 units) are replaced by an alternative pair or discarded.

Comparison genomes. Amplicon windows are aligned (BLAST, ≥ 85 % identity, affine gaps) to every comparison genome. The allele is the length of the repeat region in that genome (forensic nomenclature, e.g. 9.3 = 9 units + 3 bp). A primer site is conserved with ≤ 2 mismatches per primer, ≤ 1 in the last five 3′ bases and the two terminal 3′ bases matching..

Criteria. See below. Markers are listed regardless of linkage and amplicon overlap – the database is a pool to build your own panel from.

In-silico PCR and multiplexing. Primers are checked for additional products in the reference and the human genome (≤ 3 mismatches, products ≤ 1,500 bp; reported, not filtered). Primer Pooler assigns markers to ≥ 2 multiplex pools, avoiding primer dimers (ΔG ≤ −7 kcal/mol) and never placing overlapping amplicons in the same pool.
