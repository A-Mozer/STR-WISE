Methods

Pipeline

1. Genomes
For each CITES-listed genus with at least one genome assembly available in NCBI, the best assembly is selected as the reference genome. Other usable assemblies from the same genus are used as comparison genomes. If the reference lacks a sex chromosome present in other assemblies (e.g. a Y chromosome in a female reference), the best available genome carrying that chromosome is used for marker discovery on that chromosome.

2. STR Detection
PERF is used to identify tetranucleotide STRs. Candidate loci must contain 7–30 repeat units and a longest perfect repeat block of at least 5 units. Compound repeats separated by gaps of ≤ 10 bp are merged. Loci are excluded if another tetranucleotide STR occurs within 100 bp or another simple sequence repeat within 20 bp.

3. Primer Design
Primer3 designs primer pairs with an expected amplicon length of 100–450 bp. Primers must be 18–27 bp long, with a melting temperature of 57–63 °C (maximum difference between primer Tm values: 3 °C) and a GC content of 30–65 %. The 3′ end of a primer must not overlap a repeat-masked region or another repeat.

4. Single-Copy Screening
Amplicon windows that produce a second alignment to the reference genome covering ≥ 50 % of the window at ≥ 85 % sequence identity are excluded. If an amplicon contains another tetranucleotide STR, an alternative primer pair is selected where available; otherwise, the locus is discarded.

5. Comparison Across Genomes
Amplicon windows are aligned to each comparison genome using BLAST, with a minimum sequence identity of 85 % and affine gap penalties. The allele is defined by the length of the repeat region in each genome, following forensic repeat nomenclature. Primer-binding sites are considered conserved if each primer has ≤ 2 mismatches, no more than 1 mismatch occurs within the last five 3′ bases, and the terminal two 3′ bases match exactly.

6. Marker Selection Criteria
Candidate markers are evaluated against the selection criteria described below. Markers are retained in the database regardless of linkage or amplicon overlap. STR-WISE therefore provides a pool of candidate markers from which users can select suitable combinations for their own panels.

7. In-Silico PCR and Multiplexing
Primer pairs are screened for potential off-target amplification products in the reference genome and the human genome. PrimerPooler is then used to assign markers to at least two multiplex pools while avoiding primer-dimer interactions and preventing overlapping amplicons from being assigned to the same pool.
