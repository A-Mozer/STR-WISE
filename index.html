Genome-informed STR marker discovery for wildlife forensics

Why does it matter?
Illegal wildlife trade threatens biodiversity and creates significant challenges for law enforcement and wildlife forensic laboratories. Genetic evidence can help establish the identity and origin of seized animals and animal-derived materials, but forensic markers are not available for most affected species. STR-WISE addresses this gap by systematically screening available genome data for variable tetranucleotide STRs. Its genus-oriented approach aims to identify candidate markers that can be applied across multiple species within a genus, helping to guide the broad development of wildlife forensic marker panels.


Applications

Once validated the STR-WISE marker can be applied in a wide range of forensic and conservation applications:

  • Illegal Wildlife Trade

    Supports the detection of illegal trade involving wildlife and related products and other derivatives.

  • Individual Identification

    Genetic fingerprinting to prove poaching and link crime scenes.

  • Pedigree Testing

    Provides genetic verification of pedigree records, supporting the authentication of identities in breeding and trade.

  • Population Assignment

    Enables the assignment of individuals or seized wildlife-derived materials to their population of origin, helping identify potential poaching hotspots and inform targeted conservation and law enforcement efforts.

  • Human Crime Investigations

    Supports criminal investigations involving wildlife-derived DNA evidence, including biological traces recovered from suspects, objects or crime scenes.

  • Genetic Diversity

    Enables the assessment of genetic diversity in wildlife populations, contributing to conservation research and population management.
